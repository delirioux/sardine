Sardine 0.8.36: live club mixing for the FM-1.

- Four layers, always there, each on or OFF: LEAD, CHORD, BASS and DRUMS. PRESETS goes from one to the next, FX
  through a layer's pages (MIX: VOLUME TYPE INSTR MUTE, ARP, PAN, PAN 3D, PUMP, GATE, MOD; DRUMS: MIX, KIT, MOD), HOME back to them all
- EDIT: a pattern editor, KNOB 1 the step, KNOB 2 ready fills (drum fills, lines in the key, chord rhythms), REC:
  the keys write the steps
- STYLE MIX (GLO held): from one style into another, the layers you don't keep fading out over 1 to 4 bars, the new
  style fading in around the kept bass or kick over 2 to 16 bars in a key next door (circle of fifths), the tempo
  gliding along, every fade from and to the volumes you set
- LIVE (GLO): a mix built by hand from the kick, a pad or nothing, a layer at a time on SEQ or every 8/16 bars
- MIDI OUT (MENU, then HOME): a channel for each layer, what goes out; with the notes each layer's volume (CC7),
  cutoff (CC74) and pan (CC10) as they move
- OCT- OCT+ EDIT REC held: WASH, THROW, TAPE STOP, WOBBLE (a tap: the button's own job)
- ENV held: a drum fill from the next beat, eight bar-long fills by turns, peaking on the bar's end, the filter dipping into the 1; ARP held: BREAK, the drums and bass out,
  back in on the 1; LFO held: SWEEP, the filter closing over two bars
- STYLE: twenty styles (NIGHT, FILTER, DEEP, TECH, MINIMAL, TECHNO, HARD, GABBER, ACID, TRANCE, PROG, DISCO, AFRO,
  GARAGE, BREAKS, DUB, AMBIENT, ELECTRO, HAPPY, LOFI), a fresh groove in one on SEQ; INTRO and ARC
- GLO: a MIXER, the four layers' volumes, mutes and solos; STYLE; SONG, eight steps of sets played in turn
- Phrases: the synth layers over four bars, A A A' B (a lift in bar 3, an answer on the root in bar 4); PART A and B,
  each layer's second line and a progression of its own, on HOME's ALGORITHM or by themselves every 8 or 16 bars; a
  badge (A>B, B>A) blinks on the beats two bars before the change, on the eighths in the last bar; EVOLVE (LIGHT,
  MORE) reworks a part while it rests; SEQ held on HOME renews the resting part at once
- SELECT is the tempo, ALGORITHM the pattern; SEQ drops what waits on the one; REC records steps on the keys
- DRUMS: seven rows on channels of their own (KICK SNARE CLAP HATS TOMS RIM PERC), each drum switched at once, in eight variations (TIGHT ROOM HIGH LOW, 80 10 DEEP);
  VOLUME and ACCENT per row, CHANCE beside the modifiers; the kit's TUNE, TONE and DECAY on its KIT page
- PUMP per layer: its depth (set by the sound's type), release, and the kick or every beat
- PAN 3D (one or the other with PAN, the other greyed): a synth layer placed around you for headphones (angle, height, distance), fixed or moving (ORBIT, RANDOM,
  FLY), drawn on a circle
- An arpeggiator on every synth layer, off until you turn it on; GATE, PAN and four modifiers on every layer
- Every start a fresh groove; SETS keeps eight grooves (LOAD on the one, STORE, NEW, CLEAR) and LAST, the last groove,
  by itself
- 104 synth sounds, among them an AMBIENT set in a big hall and thirteen leads made for the styles (every style two
  leads and two lead lines of its own, the singing ones with a vibrato) and ten wild RAVE leads that stab, gate,
  sweep and scream in time; 24 synthesised drum kits (HYPNO, RUMBLE, PUNCH, SOFT: the kick on each chord's root, taken by the
  styles that suit it); 24 drum and 38 synth patterns
- On Felucca 1.8.2-beta: newer FM-1 units' flash is recognised

Install it from the site with a Chromium browser (Chrome, Edge) and a USB cable. The package also holds three files of
JieLi's AC79 SDK under Apache-2.0: see LICENSING.md.
