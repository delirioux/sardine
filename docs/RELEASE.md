Sardine 1.0.6: live club mixing for the FM-1. Everything planned is in; from here on it is listening and tuning.

1.0.1: the PUMP you hear: its dip in dB (down to -18 dB), held 30 ms on the kick, back like a sidechain's release.

1.0.2: MIDI clock in and out (MENU's CLOCK: INT sends it, EXT follows it); STYLE MIX from stopped plays the new
style, fading it in; PHRASE, A/B and EVOLVE stay on through new grooves and styles.

1.0.3: PHRASE, A/B, EVOLVE, ARC and MANUAL taken as a new groove comes in on its 1, so turned on while it waits
they stay on too.

1.0.4: A/B and EVOLVE kept over power-off.

1.0.5: the power-on LED test runs the keys from both ends to the middle.

1.0.6: WASH and THROW held together while playing no longer drop into the update mode (it looked like a crash);
OCT- and OCT+ held 5 s go there only when stopped, counted down on the screen.

- Four layers, always there, each on or OFF: LEAD, CHORD, BASS and DRUMS. PRESETS goes from one to the next, FX
  through a layer's pages (MIX: VOLUME TYPE INSTR MUTE, MANUAL, ARP, PAN, PAN 3D, PUMP, GATE, MOD; DRUMS: MIX, MANUAL, KIT, MOD), HOME back to them all
- MANUAL (a page on every layer): the layer played by hand, nothing automatic on it (no PHRASE, no B line, no
  EVOLVE or NEW, not held out by INTRO, ARC or LIVE); nothing cleared, it plays on as it is
- HOME VIEW (HOME again on HOME): a scope for LEAD, CHORD, BASS and the drums, and what comes by itself (ARC
  small, or the next 16 bars: phrases, PART's turn, INTRO, LIVE, STYLE MIX)
- An LED test round at power-on: every LED in turn under the logo, then all at once
- Hats that hiss, not ring: the 10's, the 77's and the 80's open hat with noise under their metal; DUB
  WOB, a raw wobbling lead for DUB; DUB SKANK made over
- MELODIES: sixteen lead lines over 2 or 4 bars, a call and its answer, in scale steps from each chord,
  two for every style; a fresh groove's LEAD mostly a sound that holds its notes
- EDIT: a pattern editor, KNOB 1 the step, KNOB 2 its length, KNOB 3 its note, KNOB 4 ready fills (a drum: KNOB 2, drum fills; lines in the key, chord rhythms), REC:
  the keys write the steps
- STYLE MIX (GLO held): from one style into another, the layers you don't keep fading out over 1 to 4 bars, the new
  style fading in around the kept bass or kick over 2 to 16 bars in a key next door (circle of fifths), the tempo
  gliding along, every fade from and to the volumes you set
- LIVE (GLO): a mix built by hand from the kick, a pad or nothing, a layer at a time on SEQ or every 8/16 bars
- MIDI OUT (MENU, then HOME): a channel for each layer, what goes out; with the notes each layer's volume (CC7),
  cutoff (CC74) and pan (CC10) as they move
- OCT- OCT+ EDIT REC held: WASH, THROW, TAPE STOP, WOBBLE (a tap: the button's own job); PLAY held: FADE, the mix out
  over a bar, then stopped
- ENV held: a drum fill from the next beat, eight bar-long fills by turns (held on: the next one every bar), peaking on the bar's end, the filter dipping into the 1; ARP held: BREAK, the drums and bass out,
  back in on the 1; LFO held: SWEEP, the filter closing over two bars
- STYLE: twenty styles (NIGHT, FILTER, DEEP, TECH, MINIMAL, TECHNO, HARD, GABBER, ACID, TRANCE, PROG, DISCO, AFRO,
  GARAGE, BREAKS, DUB, AMBIENT, ELECTRO, HAPPY, LOFI), a fresh groove in one on SEQ; INTRO and ARC (its build ending in WOBBLE, WASH on its last bar; drawn on the STYLE page, the bars to the drop)
- GLO: a MIXER, the four layers' volumes, mutes and solos; STYLE; SONG, eight steps of sets played in turn
- Phrases: the synth layers over four bars, A A A' B (a lift in bar 3, an answer on the root in bar 4); PART A and B,
  each layer's second line and a progression of its own, on HOME's ALGORITHM or by themselves every 8 or 16 bars; a
  badge (A>B, B>A) blinks on the beats two bars before the change, on the eighths in the last bar; EVOLVE (LIGHT,
  MORE) reworks a part while it rests; SEQ held on HOME renews the resting part at once
- SELECT is the tempo, ALGORITHM the pattern; SEQ drops what waits on the one; REC records steps on the keys
- DRUMS: seven rows on channels of their own (KICK SNARE CLAP HATS TOMS RIM PERC), each drum switched at once, in eight variations (TIGHT ROOM HIGH LOW, 80 10 DEEP);
  VOLUME and ACCENT per row, CHANCE beside the modifiers; the kit's TUNE, TONE and DECAY on its KIT page
- PUMP per layer: its depth (set by the sound's type), release, and the kick or every beat
- PAN 3D (one or the other with PAN, the other greyed): a synth layer placed around you for headphones (angle, height, distance), fixed or moving (ORBIT, RANDOM,
  FLY), drawn on a circle
- An arpeggiator on every synth layer, off until you turn it on; GATE, PAN and four modifiers on every layer
- Every start a fresh groove; SETS keeps eight grooves (LOAD on the one, STORE, NEW, CLEAR) and LAST, the last groove,
  by itself
- 145 synth sounds, among them an AMBIENT set in a big hall, a bass and a chord sound made for each style, thirteen
  leads made for the styles (every style two
  leads and two lead lines of its own, the singing ones with a vibrato) and ten wild RAVE leads that stab, gate,
  sweep and scream in time; 24 synthesised drum kits (HYPNO, RUMBLE, PUNCH, SOFT: the kick on each chord's root, taken by the
  styles that suit it); 24 drum and 38 synth patterns
- On [Felucca](https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca) 1.8.2-beta: newer FM-1 units' flash is recognised

Install it from the site with a Chromium browser (Chrome, Edge) and a USB cable. The package also holds three files of
JieLi's AC79 SDK under Apache-2.0: see LICENSING.md.
