# Sardine: quick cheat sheet

Every way to change Sardine's sound, and how to get there. 46 in all: 19 ways to play music, 3 instruments, 23
effects and 1 volume. The [manual](MANUAL.md) has the details.

**Getting around:** PRESETS steps through HOME, LEAD, CHORD, BASS and DRUMS. FX steps through a layer's pages. HOME
takes you back from anywhere. Changes to a pattern wait, shown in the second colour, until **SEQ** drops them in on
the next bar.

## Play music (19)

| | What | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Keys | On a layer, the keys play it live (on HOME: LEAD; on DRUMS: the drum you picked). They play the notes as you press them; MENU's **SNAP** (HOME held, KNOB 3) snaps them to the scale |
| 2 | Pattern | **ALGORITHM** on a layer: one of 38 synth or 24 drum patterns; **SEQ** drops it in on the next bar |
| 3 | EDIT | **EDIT** or **REC** on a layer: KNOB 1 the step, a key or KNOB 4 the note, KNOB 3 its length, KNOB 2 a ready fill. On a drum, KNOB 4 solos it while you edit |
| 4 | ARP | FX to **ARP**: MODE (10), RATE, RANGE, GATE |
| 5 | MOD | FX to **MOD**: 10 modifiers that bend a pattern (FAST, SLOW, REV, EVERY, THIN, EUCLID, SHIFT, LEN, OCT, PLY) |
| 6 | Harmony | **SCL**: KEY, SCALE (4), CHORDS (10 progressions), BARS per chord |
| 7 | Tempo | **SELECT**, from any page |
| 8 | FEEL (swing) | HOME, KNOB 2 |
| 9 | PHRASE | HOME, FX twice (PARTS), KNOB 1: ON or OFF |
| 10 | PART A/B | **ALGORITHM** on HOME cues a part; HOME PARTS, KNOB 2: **A/B** by itself every 8 or 16 bars |
| 11 | EVOLVE / NEW | HOME PARTS, KNOB 3: each part reworked while it rests. **SEQ** held on HOME: the resting part all new |
| 12 | Fill | Hold **ENV** |
| 13 | BREAK | Hold **ARP**; let go on the 1 for the drop |
| 14 | MUTE / SOLO | **GLO** to the MUTE or SOLO page |
| 15 | New groove / STYLE | **SEQ** held (away from HOME): a whole new groove. **GLO** to STYLE: pick one of 20, **SEQ** |
| 16 | INTRO / ARC | STYLE page, KNOB 2 and KNOB 3 |
| 17 | LIVE | **GLO** to LIVE: STYLE, START, BUILD; **SEQ** brings in one layer at a time |
| 18 | STYLE MIX | Hold **GLO**: TO, KEEP, OUT, IN; **SEQ** blends into the next style |
| 19 | SONG | **GLO** to SONG: steps of saved sets and their bars; SONG ON |

## Instruments (3)

| | What | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | Synth sound | On a layer, FX to **MIX**: TYPE, then INSTR. 144 sounds: 37 bass, 37 keys, 25 pads, 39 leads, 6 FX |
| 21 | Drum instrument | DRUMS: KNOB 1 the row, KNOB 2 the instrument. 17 drum instruments with 8 variations each |
| 22 | Kit | DRUMS, FX to **KIT**, KNOB 1. 24 kits |

## Volume (1)

| | What | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 46 | VOLUME | **GLO**: the MIXER, every layer's level and the drums'; or a layer's MIX page, KNOB 1 |

Held buttons act on any page; a tap still does the button's own job.
